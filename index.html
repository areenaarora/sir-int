Bihar's deleted voters, by reason and age

A searchable table of deletions across Assembly Constituencies. Filter, sort and scan where “Deceased”, “Multiple_Enrolment”, and other reasons affect different age groups.

Data source: Election Commission of India· Published: August 30, 2025

Jump to table ↓

Bihar’s voter rolls are being updated ahead of the next polls. This interactive compiles every entry marked “deleted” in the latest constituency-wise rolls and breaks them down by reason—such as Deceased, Duplicate enrolment and Permanently shifted—and by age group. The aim is to show where cleanup is concentrated and which age bands are affected more.

Use Map view to see deletions by constituency and switch the metric between total deletions and female-vs-male %. Filter by Reason or Age group, and hover for exact counts. In Table view, search, sort and customise columns. Labels match the state roll; minor wording has been standardised for clarity.
